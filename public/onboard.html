3Credentialing info

🗂 CAQH credentials To do
CAQH is the database insurance companies pull from when credentialing. Worked at another agency before? You likely have a login (reset the password if they managed it). Never had one? Create a profile at proview.caqh.org and make it as complete as you can.
🔢 NPI number To do
Find yours at the NPI Registry (search "Individual" with your name). Don't have one? Register at nppes.cms.hhs.gov. Stuck? Call the NPI Enumerator at 1-800-465-3203.
🧒 CANS certification To do
Required if you'll see clients 20 or younger. Already certified? Enter your CANS key. Not yet? Start the certification here ("New CANS user") — keep your key safe once issued.
✍️ Professional bio for the website To do
Written in the third person — how you practice, the populations you work with, your specialties. Need inspiration? Browse our team's bios.
